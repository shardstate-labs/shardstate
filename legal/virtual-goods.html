Virtual Goods Terms

Last updated: May 1, 2026

In-Game Purchases

SHARDSTATE is a browser card game. Purchases may unlock virtual currency, card packs, battle pass access, cosmetics, account progression, or other in-game content.

Closed Game Economy

  • FLUX, SHARDS, cards, cosmetics, rewards, and battle pass items are virtual goods for use only inside SHARDSTATE.
  • Virtual goods are gameplay entitlements for entertainment use only.
  • Virtual goods cannot be redeemed for money.
  • Virtual goods are non-transferable outside the game account system.
  • SHARDSTATE does not operate an external resale market for virtual goods.

Delivery

When a purchase is approved, SHARDSTATE grants the purchased content to the buyer's game account using server-side fulfillment. Delivery is normally immediate, but delays may occur due to payment review, network issues, or maintenance.

Refunds

Refund handling depends on the payment provider and applicable consumer rules. If purchased content was not delivered, contact support with your account email, purchase date, product, and payment receipt.
